My story

I'm a UI/UX designer at the start of my career, with a background in Business Information Systems at Babeș-Bolyai University (FSEGA). I discovered my interest in digital interface design during a front-end project there — and the feedback I got made me realize I'm drawn not just to the technical side, but to how design shapes the experience people have with a product.

I'm passionate about design, psychology, and the way people interact with digital products. I love creating clean, intuitive, easy-to-use interfaces that solve real problems and feel good to use.

I believe good design isn't just a product that looks good — it's one that helps people reach their goals without effort or frustration.

What I do

I take a product from a fuzzy idea to a clear, buildable interface:

  • Research & discovery — interviews, surveys, and synthesis with personas & journey maps.
  • Structure — user flows, information architecture, and wireframes.
  • Interface — high-fidelity UI and consistent design systems in Figma.
  • Prototype & test — clickable prototypes and usability testing to validate ideas.
