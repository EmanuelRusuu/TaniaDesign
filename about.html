My story

I came to design because I love the moment a confusing screen suddenly makes sense. After completing an intensive UX/UI program with ITSCHOOL, I've been sharpening my process on self-directed projects — the two case studies you'll find in my work.

I don't have years of agency experience yet, and I think that's a strength right now: I ask a lot of questions, I sweat the details, and I'm genuinely excited to learn from a team.

What I do

I take a product from fuzzy idea to a clear, buildable interface:

  • Research & discovery — user interviews, competitive teardowns, defining the real problem.
  • Structure — user flows, information architecture, low-fi wireframes.
  • Interface — high-fidelity UI, reusable components, and tidy design systems in Figma.
  • Prototype & test — clickable prototypes to validate ideas before a line of code is written.
