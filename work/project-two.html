Project Two — rethinking the [experience]

A redesign concept that brings clarity and flow to [product], making it easier for [audience] to [achieve goal].

Project Two
My role
UX & UI Design
Timeline
[e.g. 4 weeks]
Tools
Figma, FigJam
Type
Redesign concept

Overview

Set the scene: what is this product today, and what made it a good candidate for a redesign? Frame the opportunity in a couple of sentences.

The challenge

What was broken or unclear in the original experience? Be specific about the pain points you noticed and who they affected.

Design question: How might we make [product] clearer and faster to use for [audience]?

Research & discovery

Explain how you evaluated the current experience — a heuristic review, a small usability test, or comparing competitors. Surface the key problems worth fixing.

  • Problem 1 — what wasn't working and the impact on users.
  • Problem 2 — a confusing pattern or flow.
  • Problem 3 — a gap versus users' expectations.

Wireframes & flow

Show how you re-thought the structure. Include the improved user flow and the wireframes that map out your proposed solution.

Final UI

Present the redesigned interface side-by-side with the original where it helps. Explain the visual system and the decisions behind it.

Outcome & reflection

Close with the impact of the redesign, what you learned, and what you'd explore next. Even for a concept, a thoughtful reflection carries a lot of weight.
